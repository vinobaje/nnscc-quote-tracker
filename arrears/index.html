Prepare a batch of letters

Paste this month's arrears list, check the rows, pick a template, and print. Every letter is recorded so the next batch knows what has already gone out.

1

Paste the arrears list

One unit per line. Unit number first, dollar amount last — a name in between is optional. Commas, tabs, dashes and $ are all fine. Copying straight out of a spreadsheet works.
Or paste just the unit numbers — 550-210, 560-811, 560-406 — and type the amounts into the table below.

These are drafted and attached but not yet sent. Review them in Gmail, then Send them all — the app sends every draft listed here, so they do not have to be opened one at a time. Check Gmail clears any that left the Drafts folder some other way; with drafts-only access the app can see a draft is gone, not whether it was sent or deleted, so a cleared row means "no longer waiting", not "proven delivered". Ones it sends itself are recorded as sent, because it did the sending.

2

Check the list

Owner names and addresses come from the Units tab. Anything not found there is flagged — fill it in here and it will be saved for next time. Click any row to see exactly what that owner would receive.

Click a unit on the left to see the email and the letter it would receive.

3

Choose the letter

Email drafts puts one draft per unit in Gmail, each with its PDF attached, and sends nothing — you read each one and press Send there. Print letters opens a printable page for the whole selection, one letter per sheet. Either way the batch is recorded in History, so the next batch knows what has already gone out.
Units

Owner of record

The name and mailing address used on every letter. Fill this in once; after that you only ever paste unit numbers and amounts.

Importing from the management system. Click Import CSVs and select the Unit Manager and User Manager exports together — the order you pick them in does not matter, they are applied in the right one. Unit Manager carries the units, owners, tenants, parking, lockers and entry instructions; User Manager carries every person with their contacts and each owner's real mailing address. You need both for a complete record. Re-importing later updates what has changed and never duplicates a unit.

One unit per line: unit, owner name, mailing address. Units already on the list are updated, not duplicated.

The letters you have already sent. Choose the spreadsheet you keep them in — .xlsx or .csv, several files or several tabs at once — or paste the rows straight out of Excel. The columns can be in any order and headings are optional; it looks for the unit numbers and works out the rest. You see every row before anything is saved, and importing the same sheet twice changes nothing.

Templates

Letter wording

Edit the wording here and it applies to every letter from then on. The {{...}} fields are filled in per unit when you generate.

The wording below is a starting draft, not approved text. Replace it with the Corporation's own letters and have counsel review anything that refers to liens, interest, costs or deadlines before it goes out. The same goes for the pre-authorized debit form — confirm the wording with the Corporation's bank.

Blank line = new paragraph. # Heading centres a title. **bold** for bold. --- draws a line. [[SIGNATURE]] and [[PAD_FORM]] drop in the signature block and the banking authorization form.

History

Every letter that has gone out

The record of what was demanded, from whom, and when. Letters cannot be edited or deleted — one sent in error is marked void and stays on the record, greyed out and no longer counted against that unit. Voiding cannot be undone.
